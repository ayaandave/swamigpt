## Description (4,000)
SwamiGPT is a calm guide to Hindu wisdom for everyday life. Ask anything, in your own words, and get practical help first, then a real verse from scripture, and what that verse means for you.

Every verse is real. SwamiGPT answers only from its own library of more than 4,000 passages from the Bhagavad Gita, the Upanishads, the Yoga Sutras, Swami Vivekananda and the Ramayana, in public-domain translations. The verse you see is quoted word for word from that library, and every one links to its own page so you can check it yourself.

You don't have to be Hindu. Ideas like "do your best and let go of the result" help anyone, from students before a test to grandparents looking for peace.

ASK
- Ask about stress, purpose, relationships, karma, God or anything on your mind
- Choose "Simple and warm" for everyday words, or "Deeper study" to compare how different schools of Hindu thought read the same verse
- Ask out loud with the microphone, and hear answers read aloud

LEARN
- 47 five-minute lessons, like "The Gita in 18 Days" and "What is karma?"
- A ring of prayer beads (a mala) fills in one bead for every lesson you finish
- Browse the whole library, verse by verse, with Sanskrit and transliteration

MEDITATE
- Guided meditations with a breathing circle and words that follow the voice
- Keeps playing with your screen locked
- Mantras and calming background sounds

EVERY DAY
- A new verse every morning with a small practice for the day
- An optional daily notification with today's verse

SAFE AND PRIVATE
- No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold
- Use it without an account, or sign up to save your conversations
- Children under 13 need a parent or guardian to make an account
- If someone seems to be in crisis, SwamiGPT points them to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

SwamiGPT is an AI guide, not a guru. It quotes real scripture and can still make mistakes. It is not a substitute for a teacher, a doctor or a counselor.

## Notes for the App Review team
SwamiGPT is a free guide to Hindu scripture. No account is needed to try it: open the Ask tab and type a question (for example "I'm stressed about a test"). Guests get a few free questions a day.

Native features to try:
- Settings (the sliders icon, top right) → "Morning verse" turns on a daily notification with that day's verse.
- Meditate → open any meditation and tap Begin, then lock the screen: the guided audio keeps playing.
- Ask → tap the microphone to ask out loud (speech recognition; the microphone is used only when tapped).
- Offline: pages you've opened stay available without internet; otherwise a calm offline screen appears.
- Account deletion: Account (sign in first) → "Delete my account" removes the account and all its data in the app.
